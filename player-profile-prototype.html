Player Profile

Dynamic right full-back with an attacking mindset, developed through previous experience as a central box-to-box midfielder and wide player. Comfortable contributing in both defensive and attacking phases, with a strong ability to win possession, support build-up play and provide width in the final third through overlapping runs and quality delivery.

Currently competing in the NSW National Premier Leagues Boys Youth League 1 with Bulls FC Academy (Macarthur FC), having made more than 150 appearances across five seasons in an A-League academy environment.

A representative futsal player and Sydney North Regional High Schools Cross Country Champion, combining excellent technical ability in tight spaces with exceptional endurance, repeat running capacity and a consistently high work rate.

About Me

My ambition has always been to become an elite student-athlete by combining outstanding academics with high-level football. I enjoy challenging myself in both environments and thrive on hard work, knowing that consistent effort and continual learning are the foundations of long-term success.

Football has been a constant throughout my life, and my development through the Bulls FC Academy from U13 to U18 has shaped me both as a player and as a person. During that time, I've had the opportunity to play across several positions—from central midfield, then winger, and now to right back. Each role has given me a different perspective on the game and helped me develop a deeper tactical understanding.

Today, I see myself as a modern attacking defender who combines the tactical awareness of a former midfielder, the attacking instincts of a winger, and the defensive discipline required of a modern fullback. I enjoy receiving the ball under pressure, helping build attacks from wide areas, and contributing at both ends of the pitch. My background has taught me to be adaptable, comfortable in possession, and to understand the responsibilities of every phase of the game.

Away from football, I attend a selective academic high school and have achieved a 1470 SAT while pursuing a strong interest in Engineering and related STEM disciplines. I believe the discipline required to balance a demanding academic workload with academy football has helped me become organised, resilient and committed to continual improvement.

Above all, I value being coachable, dependable and putting the team first. I enjoy learning, welcome constructive feedback and believe that every training session and every match is an opportunity to improve. My goal is to join a university where I can continue to develop as both a footballer and a student, contributing to a successful team while preparing for a meaningful career beyond the game.
